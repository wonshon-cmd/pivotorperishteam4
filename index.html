    How to read the game dashboard
    • Kahn matrix: our score vs the leader. Within 5% of the leader = leading; above 75% = fair value.
    • Preferences: what each segment cares about (bigger number = cares more).
    • Market size: how many shoppers each segment has. Growing = future sales.
    • Market revenue / CLV: our sales and customer value by segment (not the whole market).
    • Product position: how shoppers see us vs rivals (0–5). Standing still = falling behind.
    • Pricing: our product cost vs rivals (shows if vendor cuts hurt).
    • Scorecard: market value = P/E × net income.
