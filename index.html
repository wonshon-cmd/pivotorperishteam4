    Getchells · Team 4 — Thesis & game plan

    Round 5 status (after our Round 4 submission). Plain-English guide to every lever, plus how we win per the professor's slides. Confidence: High clean test / exact data · Medium model estimate · Low untested guess.
    Thesis: Be the premium, wide-assortment department store. Lead Brand (quality) and Experience (shopping experience + service), stay at fair value in Low Price (markups + vendor), and accept a small Frictionless gap because closing it costs more than it's worth. Market value = P/E × net income, so grow revenue with assortment, keep spending flat, and let debt keep falling.
